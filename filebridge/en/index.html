MADE FOR EVERYDAY FILES

A simple tool for everyday files.

No cable and no desktop installation. Use a familiar browser to manage your files and photos.

01

Files, neatly organized

Upload and download app files, create folders, rename, move and extract archives from your computer. Import and preview common files on iPhone.

02

Photos, on a bigger screen

With permission, browse, filter and download photos and videos in your browser, or upload images to Photos. Photo deletion requires confirmation on iPhone.

03

You control the connection

A four-digit access code, browser read-only permissions, automatic locking and session revocation. Stop sharing to disconnect browser sessions.

THREE SIMPLE STEPS

From phone to computer in three steps.

01

Join the same Wi-Fi

Connect your iPhone and computer to the same trusted network.

02

Start sharing on iPhone

Open FileBridge, tap Start Sharing, and keep the app in the foreground.

03

Open the address and enter the code

In your computer browser, open the full address shown in the app and enter its current four-digit access code.

Local transfers use HTTP and are not encrypted in transit. Use only a trusted Wi-Fi network and stop sharing when finished.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your files stay in your hands.

FileBridge does not operate a file-upload server or collect files, photos or usage analytics. Transfers take place between your iPhone and an authorized browser on your local network.

If an original is stored only in iCloud, Apple Photos may download it when you request it. Preview compatibility depends on the file format, codec and system support.
