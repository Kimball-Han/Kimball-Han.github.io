Privacy Policy

Kim Games does not collect personal data, track you, or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, high scores, saved sessions, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this information. Removing the app may remove locally stored data.

Apple Game Center

Selected games offer optional Game Center features. When you choose them, Apple may process your Game Center player identity, matchmaking information, and match data under Apple’s privacy policy and your Game Center settings. Kim Games receives only the information needed to run the selected match and does not operate a separate account system or server.

Support email

If you email support, the developer receives the email address and message content you choose to provide. This information is used only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the app, and is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or legal obligations.

Children, security, and changes

Kim Games is a general-audience collection and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Reasonable safeguards are used for support communications. Material policy changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

隐私政策

Kim Games 不收集个人数据，不跟踪用户，也不使用广告或分析 SDK。

设备本地数据

游戏进度、最高分、保存的对局和偏好设置可能存储在你的设备本地。开发者不会收到这些信息。移除 App 时，本地数据可能同时被删除。

Apple Game Center

部分游戏提供可选的 Game Center 功能。当你主动使用这些功能时，Apple 可能依据其隐私政策和你的 Game Center 设置处理玩家身份、匹配信息与对局数据。Kim Games 仅接收完成所选对局所需的信息，不运营独立账号系统或服务器。

支持邮件

如果你向技术支持发送邮件，开发者会收到你主动提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容。这些信息仅用于回复、排查问题和改进 App，并只在上述目的或法律义务合理需要的期限内保留。

儿童、安全与政策变更

Kim Games 是面向普通用户的游戏合集，不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。开发者会对支持沟通采取合理的安全措施。若政策发生重大变化，本页面将更新生效日期并公布新内容。
